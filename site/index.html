02 WHY KAIRO EXISTS

⌁

THE MACHINE
IS YOURS TO SHAPE.

Dotfiles should feel
like someone made them.

Not a pile of copied configs. Not a black box that takes over your home folder. Kairo is a hand-built Arch setup: each config has a point of view, and every install stays in your hands.

01 / MAKE

Written with intention.

Shells, editor, terminal and desktop settings are maintained as real configurations—not generated from a template.

02 / CHOOSE

Take the parts you like.

Install one module, a full desktop, or a toolchain. Your choices are separate and easy to review.

03 / KEEP

Your machine stays yours.

Backups, staged installs and rollback protect the setup you already have.

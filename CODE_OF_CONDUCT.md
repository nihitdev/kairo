# Code of Conduct

Kairo welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, identities,
abilities, and levels of experience. Treat people with respect in issues, pull
requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Make room for questions and people learning the tools.
- Respect others' time, boundaries, and privacy.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work and follow moderator directions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual
attention, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. Neither
are deliberate disruption, spam, or retaliation against someone who raises a
concern.

Do not publish another person's private information or private communications
without permission.

## Reporting a concern

For behavior visible in a project thread, you may ask a maintainer to moderate
it. Do not repeat abusive content or expose someone else's private information.

For a sensitive concern, email `code.nihit.dev` with the subject **Kairo conduct
report**. Do not include private details in the subject. If your mail client
cannot send to that address, use the [issue tracker](https://github.com/nihitdev/kairo/issues)
to request a private contact method; do not include names, incident details, or
evidence in that public request.

Once a private channel is available, share the relevant context and links, and
explain what help you need. Maintainers should limit disclosure of report details
to what is necessary to address the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. GitHub's platform reporting tools are also available for violations
of its rules, including concerns involving a project maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, lock a discussion,
restrict participation, or ban a participant based on the severity and context
of the behavior. Serious threats or harassment may lead to immediate removal.

If you believe a moderation decision was mistaken, request reconsideration with
new context through the private contact method provided for your report. Do not
restart the dispute across unrelated threads.

These expectations apply to maintainers as well as other participants, and to
public conduct when someone is representing the project.
